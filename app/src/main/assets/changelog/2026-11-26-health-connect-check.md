See what your ring or watch writes to Health Connect

Setup → Data → Health Connect lists the sleep, heart rate and HRV records that apps like Oura have written in the last 7 days: which app wrote them, how many, the newest one, and whether sleep comes with stages. It's the first step toward showing last night's sleep in Diurna.

Diurna only reads, and only when you tap Read. The list has counts and times, not your readings, and nothing is kept or uploaded. Copy as text puts it on the clipboard if you want to send it with a bug report.
