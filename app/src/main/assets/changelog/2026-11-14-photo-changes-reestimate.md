Changing a food's photos updates its estimate

Adding, removing or reordering any of a food's photos now gets it a fresh AI estimate, since the estimate looks at all of them. Snap the label after logging something and the numbers update. Foods logged from a saved meal keep their exact numbers when you only change photos. With debug logging on, a report shows why an entry was re-estimated and how many photos were added, removed or moved (only counts, never the photos).
