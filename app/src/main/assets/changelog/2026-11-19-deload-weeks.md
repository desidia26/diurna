Deload weeks

Tap "Deload week" at the top of any week on the Week tab to mark it as lighter on purpose. That week's workouts aren't used as "last time" afterwards, so the hints and set counts for the next week pick up from your numbers before the deload. Its days say "Deload week" under the date, and on Metrics its volume bar is drawn as an outline and left out of "vs earlier weeks". Tap it again to unmark.
