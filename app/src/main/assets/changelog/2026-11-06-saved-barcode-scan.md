Clearer barcode scans

Food barcodes now scan a saved photo, with five cleanup retries and on-device reading of the printed numbers (Google Play Services OCR, downloaded at install) when the bars won't read. Tap the preview to focus, or use Capture barcode and the light toggle. Check any text-recognition suggestion before looking up the product. The app now tells a photo it couldn't read apart from a product it couldn't find, a lookup connection problem, or a text model that is still downloading.
