Each package in a photo is now read on its own

When a photo shows two or more packages, the app first finds each package on your phone (nothing is uploaded or downloaded for this step) and cuts it out of the full-size photo. Each close-up is then read in two steps: first which product it is, then its barcode or name is looked up in USDA and Open Food Facts, and the label is read knowing what those say. Numbers on the label still win. Where the label and a database disagree, the details show both. Anything the AI only guessed is listed as a guess and not used. A photo of one package is read as before.

On a counter photo of five packages, every package came back with the right numbers in all eleven test runs; before, about half did. Reading a photo now takes a few seconds longer (two AI requests per package).
