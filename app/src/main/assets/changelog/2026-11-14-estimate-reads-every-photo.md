Estimates read every photo of an entry

When a food has more than one photo, the AI now looks at all of them (up to 5), not just the first. Snap the front, the barcode and the Nutrition Facts label of a package and the estimate uses the label's numbers. For example, "3 links" with a 60-calorie-per-link label now comes out near 180 calories instead of a guess from the barcode side. With debug logging on, a report also shows how many photos went with each estimate (only the count, never the photos).
