Setup is easier to find your way around

Setup now opens on five groups: Alarm, Food, Training, Check-in & steps, and Data. Each row shows how things stand, like when the next alarm is or when the last backup ran. Tap one to see its settings, and Back takes you up a level.

When something stops the alarm or puts your data at risk, like a missing permission, an unscanned porch code or backups that have stopped, it shows at the top under Needs attention with a button to fix it.

Some things moved: Detailed nutrition is under Food, Cardio exercises under Training, and Lock screen, What's new and Debug reports under Data.
