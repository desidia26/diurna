Several packages in one photo, with questions and a decision log

Package photo now reads every package in the picture. Each one gets its own row with its sources, disagreements and its own grams or servings, and you can edit or remove a row. Save adds them all at once. Barcodes are matched to their packages.

When a package has no readable panel or barcode, Diurna names it from the front and searches USDA branded foods (with your key) and Open Food Facts. Matches are marked Guess and are only used after you pick one.

If something is unclear, like a Dry and a Prepared column, the AI asks you. Answer or type a correction to refine it. Each refinement is a new revision, so you can see what changed and go back to the original at any time.

Decisions shows which sources were used, which value won for each field and why, and what you answered or changed. It is saved with the ingredient and you can open it from the recipe line later.
