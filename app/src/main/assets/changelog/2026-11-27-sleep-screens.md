Last night on the Day screen

With Sleep on in Setup, the Morning card has a Sleep row: how long you slept, a strip of your sleep stages, and when you went to bed, woke and got up. Tap it for the whole night: a stage chart, deep, light and REM, how long it took to fall asleep, efficiency, wakes, and heart rate and HRV through the night.

Week shows hours asleep for each day, and Metrics has a Sleep card: sleep by stage each night, bedtime and wake time against your alarm and porch scan, and overnight heart rate and HRV over time. With Sleep off, nothing changes.
