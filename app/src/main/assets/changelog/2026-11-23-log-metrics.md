Log metrics: note each time something happens

A check-in metric can now be a log, for things like headaches or other symptoms. Add one in Setup → Check-in and pick the Log type.

On the Day screen, Log now records the time in one tap, and a note can follow. Tap an entry to change its time or note, or to delete it. On an earlier day, Add entry asks for the time. A log never holds up the evening reminder: no entries is a real answer.

Metrics shows a card per log with the entries per day (per week past 4 weeks) and how many a week on average.
