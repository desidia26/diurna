Sleep from your ring or watch

Setup has a new Sleep page. Turn it on and Diurna reads your sleep from Health Connect, where Oura and most rings and watches write it. It reads when you open the app, and every hour when Health Connect allows reading in the background. If more than one app writes sleep, pick which one counts.

The Morning card then shows last night: how long you slept, a strip of your sleep stages, and when you went to bed, woke and got up. Tap it for the whole night: a stage chart, deep, light and REM, how long it took to fall asleep, efficiency, wakes, and heart rate and HRV through the night. Week shows hours asleep for each day, and Metrics has a Sleep card with sleep by stage, bedtime and wake time against your alarm and porch scan, and overnight heart rate and HRV over time.

It's off by default, and only reads. Everything stays on your phone with your other Diurna data and backups. To check what your ring or watch writes before turning it on, see Setup → Data → Health Connect.
