Sleep from your ring or watch

Setup has a new Sleep page. Turn it on and Diurna reads your sleep from Health Connect, where Oura and most rings and watches write it: when you went to bed, when you woke, how long you slept, your sleep stages, and heart rate and HRV through the night. It reads when you open the app, and every hour when Health Connect allows reading in the background.

It's off by default, and only reads. Everything stays on your phone with your other Diurna data and backups. Pick which app's sleep counts if more than one writes it. Last night on the Day screen, and sleep in Week and Metrics, are coming next.
