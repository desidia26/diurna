A tidier food card

Each entry is now one line: time, name and calories, with "~" on AI estimates. Tap an entry to see its macros, what the estimate assumed, its photos, and Edit, Save as meal or Delete. Swipe an entry left to delete it, and tap Undo if you didn't mean to. Photos and barcode scans share one + button in the food box, and each attached photo has its own ×.
