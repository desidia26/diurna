Photos of five or more packages no longer get cut off

A package photo with many packages in it could fail with "The estimate was cut off" when using Grok or a custom OpenAI-compatible provider, because the reply had too little room for every package. Package reads now get enough room for a full photo.

The AI is also told more firmly not to fill in a Nutrition Facts panel it can't see (a front label or barcode is not a panel) and not to guess a brand when a package's front is turned away. Check each row before saving: on a cluttered photo it can still misread small print, like a serving size.
