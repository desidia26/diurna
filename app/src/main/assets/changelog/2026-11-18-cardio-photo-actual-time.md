Cardio photos log the time you actually took

Logging a workout from a photo of its summary screen no longer fills in the workout's planned length (like "22 MIN" in a class's title) as your time. The time comes only from the screen's own time tile, seconds included, so 19:26 stays 19:26. If that tile is cut off or unreadable, Time is left blank and the check step asks you to enter it.
