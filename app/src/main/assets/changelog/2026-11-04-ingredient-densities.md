Recipes fill in density automatically

Imported recipes used to leave volume and "each" ingredients blocked when density was missing, so nutrition stayed empty. Diurna now looks up grams per milliliter (and grams per item) from USDA FoodData Central when you have a key, falls back to a short AI estimate when you do not, and remembers the answer — including the density itself — across recipes and amount edits. When the USDA food is a good name match, empty ingredient nutrition is filled from that food too. AI estimates are marked as estimates; anything you type by hand stays yours.
