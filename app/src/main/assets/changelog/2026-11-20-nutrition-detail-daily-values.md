Clearer nutrition detail, and % Daily Value for the day

With Detailed nutrition on, an open food entry now shows protein, carbs and fat as tiles with how its calories split between them, then labelled Carbs, Fats and Vitamins & minerals sections. Amino acids fold to one line until you tap them.

The Food card has a new Nutrition detail line, for example "8 of 11 met · sodium over". Tap it to see three groups: Get enough (what you're furthest short on comes first), Keep under (sodium, saturated fat and added sugars) and Breakdown. Tap a group for its bars, and tap a nutrient to see which entries it came from. Switch between amounts and % Daily Value at the top; it remembers your choice.

Setup → Library picks what the Daily Values are: the FDA label (2,000 kcal), the label scaled to your calorie target, or recommended amounts for your age and sex. Vitamins and minerals only come from scanned or USDA-matched foods, so the card says how many entries they're known for.
