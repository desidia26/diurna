Cholesterol is in Keep under, and a heavy meal flags more than sodium

Cholesterol now has a Daily Value of 300 mg — the amount on US food labels — and sits in Keep under with sodium, saturated fat and added sugars. It shows a percentage, a bar, and turns orange if you go over. The 300 mg limit is the same if you use the FDA label, scale to your calorie target, or pick amounts by age and sex: it does not change with calories or age.

On an open food entry, if that one meal is already half a day's limit of sodium, saturated fat, added sugars or cholesterol, an orange note says so.

Setup now says that "By age and sex" uses the FDA numbers under age 19.
